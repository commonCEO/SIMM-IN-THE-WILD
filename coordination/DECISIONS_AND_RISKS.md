## Forecast rule

16-24week planning envelope has low confidence until two accepted asset packets and a current parity audit. Track accepted packets/cycle, reviewer turnaround, reopen rate, remaining campaign journeys and open high bugs. Do not measure progress by revision count, tokens spent, triangle count or heartbeat frequency.

## Escalation

Only ask the user for consequential design ambiguity, scope/release decisions or final art checkpoints. Batch questions with concrete options/evidence. Failures of a specific authorization layer remain failures; no UI impersonation or credential workaround. Routine reversible production choices are delegated.
