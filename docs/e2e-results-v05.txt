Layout checks inspect all recipe rows, their SVG art/text bounds, inter-row
intersections, horizontal overflow and panel intersections. Resolutions:
1280x720, 1920x1080 and 3840x2160, in both supported languages.

Model checks cover enlarged biome areas and mountain heights; all six NPC ages,
names and quest transactions; useful enemy drops; cloth-to-fibre output; healing
salve; bow/charm effects; deterministic rare events; one-time gold claims; bag
overflow; lantern costs; legacy save/boat migration; and the cave approach.

Legacy scenarios retain resource gathering, hunting, combat, cooking, fishing,
save/reload, defeat recovery, skills, crop growth, NPC rewards, houses/interiors,
stairs, storage, boats/whales, lamps, camera directions and terrain movement.
World-size and new layout assumptions in old tests were updated explicitly.

Limitations: controller input is simulated through the Gamepad API; no physical
Xbox controller or console was available. Tests are automated heuristic and
functional checks, not a controlled human usability study or exhaustive review
of every route/building layout. Managed Chromium blocks direct file:// URLs;
that part explicitly skips, while inline and local-HTTP standalone builds pass.
NPCs currently use full-body idle sprites/portraits and text dialogue; this
release does not introduce recorded voices or walking animation sprite sheets.
Research and implementation rationale: docs/v05-design-notes.txt.
