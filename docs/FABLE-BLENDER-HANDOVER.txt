You are the Blender artist for SIMM-IN-THE-WILD, a cozy woodland adventure
co-designed with a child. Use Blender through your MCP connection to create
REAL 3D assets for the new Godot 4 prototype. Work in the existing repository:

VISUAL TARGET — THIS IS THE MAIN ACCEPTANCE CRITERION
Read docs/v06-art-directions.png and use ONLY panel 2 (upper right), or use the
large crop attached by the user. The reference shows a sculpted, warm, tactile
forest diorama: rounded foliage clusters, dimensional characters with expressive
faces, natural wood/cloth/leather, soft warm sunlight and coherent contact shadows.
Use docs/npc-concepts-v2.png for the approved character identities.
The current browser screenshot docs/v06-gameplay.png is the FAILED approximation,
not the style target. Do not reproduce its flat sprite presentation.

Make actual geometry that looks dimensional from several camera angles. No
whole-object billboards, flat cutout characters, painted floor shadows, foliage
shaped like cardboard, ground discs attached to feet, or baked depth-of-field.
Avoid shiny plastic, extreme low-poly faceting and photorealistic skin. Use soft
bevels, smooth normals, warm restrained colors and a consistent level of detail.
The generated reference is art direction, not a texture to project onto planes.

SCOPE — ONE PLAYABLE CLEARING FIRST
Read godot/assets/manifest.json. It is the shared asset contract: filenames,
approximate dimensions in metres, model kinds and required animation names.
Build a cohesive starter kit of these 13 assets, not the entire game:
1. simm.glb: brown-haired young explorer, yellow jacket, olive backpack, dark
   trousers, brown boots. Height about 1.35 m. Friendly expressive face.
2. leina.glb: gardener, 13, auburn ponytail, yellow shirt and coral overalls,
   watering can and flowers. Height about 1.50 m.
3. bronkings.glb: blacksmith, 22, BALD, broad leather apron with pockets and
   tools, hammer, fair and approachable expression. Height about 1.75 m.
4–13. workbench, tent, oak, pine, birch, rock, berry_bush, branch, pebble,
   grass_clump. Exact .glb filenames and dimensions are in the manifest.

Make Simm, an oak and the workbench first to establish consistent proportions,
material treatment and detail, then carry that language across the starter kit.
Keep props recognizable at an elevated gameplay distance. The workbench should
read as wood with small metal tools, not an untextured cube. Trees need a visible
trunk and volumetric canopies. The tent is tan cloth with simple poles and vines.

BLENDER / GODOT EXPORT CONTRACT
- 1 Blender unit = 1 metre. Metric, unit scale 1.0. Follow manifest sizes within
  roughly 10%; do not silently use centimetres or compensate with export scale.
- Ground/foot pivot at origin. Characters stand on Blender Z=0. Center horizontally.
- Blender up +Z, character front -Y; standard glTF Y-up conversion makes Godot
  up +Y and model front +Z. The Godot movement code uses that model convention.
- Each exported root is named after its asset ID with identity transform.
  Static object transforms applied; rigs authored at correct scale before binding.
- Export one asset per self-contained GLB to godot/assets/models/<id>.glb.
  Use glTF 2.0 binary, Y-up, embedded textures, exported skin/animation where needed.
  Exclude Blender cameras, lights, studio ground, preview rigs and other assets.
- Keep editable source files under art_source/blender/ and shared textures under
  art_source/textures/. Do not overwrite existing .blend work or unrelated files.
- Use glTF-compatible PBR materials. Bake unsupported procedural Blender nodes
  to base-color/roughness/normal textures; verify the GLB, not just the .blend.
  Avoid duplicated huge textures; 1K/2K textures and shared materials are sufficient
  for this initial scene where appropriate. Do not bake illumination or shadows.
- Approximate budgets, subordinate to silhouette/quality: characters 8k–20k
  triangles, trees 3k–12k, props 500–8k. Report actual counts and material counts.
- Simm animations: idle, walk, gather. NPC animations: idle, talk. Exact lower-case
  clip names, in-place locomotion, no drifting root, idle/walk loops without pops.
  Keep rig/skin data exportable to Godot's Skeleton3D/AnimationPlayer.
- Include a right-hand attachment node named hand_r_socket for future held tools
  and document its hierarchy. Skinning must not stretch during walking/gathering.
- Do not generate whole-canopy collision meshes. The Godot scaffold owns physics:
  tree collision is the trunk; loose pickups have no blocking collision. If an
  asset needs a different footprint, report it in an asset report rather than
  embedding invisible large blockers or silently changing the manifest.
