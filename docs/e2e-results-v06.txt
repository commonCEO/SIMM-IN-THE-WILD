FIXES FOUND DURING THIS RELEASE'S TESTING
- A dungeon coordinate was missing from the standalone export wrapper, freezing
  the simulation on cave entry. Export handling and build-time validation fixed.
- Resource placement could cover the volcano entrance and win interaction
  selection. The entrance is cleared and the volcanic biome uses sparse ore/rock
  scenery instead of a dense green forest.
- NPC quest/house actions could share a row, preventing down-navigation to the
  housing button. They now form a vertical controller-accessible list.
- Focused primary actions could have dark text on a dark background. Contrast fixed.
- Outdoor category state could leave the indoor build view empty. Categories are
  validated per scene; indoor building lists appropriate furnishings.
- New coastline positions could leave boat placement too far from land. Boat
  placement/movement now follows the actual shoreline rather than a fixed x line.
- Home plot and doorway are protected from later placement; yard clearing and
  regrowth rules keep the approach clear. Older occupied plots seek a free site.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
The main scenario suite uses seeded initial saves to isolate systems and keep
runs repeatable; it is not a claim that the entire campaign was completed from a
single fresh save. The separate recipe/collision run genuinely gathers and crafts
its starting supplies through the UI. House waits use the normal rest mechanic,
not a test-only clock advance. Physical Xbox hardware was not attached: controller
checks simulate the browser Gamepad API. Art is a 2.5D diorama rendering, not a
polygonal 3D engine. Multiplayer remains explicitly deferred.
